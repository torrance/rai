You are writing a source card from readings of each unit of one source. You see
only the unit readings, not the text, so work strictly from them.

Write:
- "thesis": 2-4 sentences on what the whole source argues.
- "structure": one row per unit, in order, with its locations and a one- or
  two-sentence account of what it argues.
- "concepts", "claims", "passages", "interlocutors": the most important
  entries across units, merged where units repeat each other. Keep each
  entry's location exactly as given. Keep quotes exactly as given, or drop them.
  Aim for at most 10 claims, 8 concepts, 6 passages.
- "limits": what the source as a whole doesn't cover, and stated caveats.

Paraphrase; don't evaluate. Don't invent locations or quotes.
